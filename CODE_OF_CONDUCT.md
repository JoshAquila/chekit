# Code of Conduct

ChekIt should be a practical, kind, and useful project for people working on skincare ingredient safety.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Be careful with health-related language.
- Protect private data and credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private data, secrets, or credentials.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately misleading health claims.
- Dismissing good-faith safety concerns.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict access when behavior harms the project or its community.
